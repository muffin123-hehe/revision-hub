🐾 Revision Hub language-learning tips

  • Learn five new words every day.
  • Practise saying complete sentences out loud.
  • Use flashcards for Spanish on one side and English on the other.
  • Learn verbs with their pronouns.
  • Review words you got wrong the next day.
